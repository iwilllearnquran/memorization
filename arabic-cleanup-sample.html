Full Ayah Comparison (IndoPak vs Uthmani)

Your local JSON uses IndoPak script. UthmanicHafs is designed for Uthmani script.
The circles in UthmanicHafs are caused by 5 IndoPak-specific characters that the font doesn't have glyphs for:
ۡ U+06E1 (IndoPak sukoon, 22× per ayah — biggest offender!) · ؕ U+0615 (waqf mark) · ۙ U+06D9 (waqf mark) · ڪ U+06AA (swash kaf, should be ك) · U+E01C (Private Use Area)
Uthmani script uses ْ U+0652 for sukoon, ك U+0643 for kaf, and different waqf marks.
Note: All three font files (local, v2, quran.com's) are byte-identical (MD5: c06c3d61).
