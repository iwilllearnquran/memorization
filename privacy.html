📜 Privacy Policy

My Quran Quest

My Quran Quest (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide you with a personalized learning experience:

  • Account Information:
    • If you sign in with Google, we collect your name and email address for account creation.
    • Guest users can use the app without providing personal details.
  • Learning Progress:
    • Last-read Surah and Ayah.
    • Points, streak history, and completed ayahs.
  • Device Information (Automatically Collected):
    • Basic technical data (device type, app version) may be collected by Firebase to ensure app stability.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (e.g., contacts, photos, location).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your Quran learning progress.
  • To show your streaks, points, and achievements.
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs.
  • To send notifications if you opt-in.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud Platform): for authentication, data storage, and analytics.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is securely stored in Google Firebase (Firestore).
  • Guest data is stored locally on your device.
  • We use encryption and industry-standard practices to protect your information.

5. Your Choices

  • You can continue as a Guest without providing personal details.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.
  • You can clear local progress stored on your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

  • The app is designed for general use and is safe for all ages.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.
