How It Works
Non-Quran mode transcribes audio to SRT with FFmpeg Whisper, then burns the subtitles into a 1080x1920 reel. Quran mode fetches Arabic ayah text and MP3 from API, generates the SRT from those timings, then renders the reel. Word Reel mode generates Arabic + English TTS audio for Quran vocabulary entries, creates matching subtitles, and renders the reel.
